Quick Start

Welcome to the Loquendon't engine. Generating high-quality audio is easy:

  1. Select a Voice from the menu and insert it.
  2. Type your script. Mix multiple voices if you want!
  3. Add Pauses to make it sound natural.
  4. Hit Generate and your file will auto-download.

Note: Long scripts may take a few seconds to process.
